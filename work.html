Work with me

Three fixed-scope engagements, each ending in a written result.

The research on this site is preregistered and reported even when it reverses. You can commission the same kind of work. Each offer below has a fixed scope, a fixed price range and a fixed deliverable. Nothing is open-ended, and no result is chosen in advance.

OFFER 01

Claim audit

Test one result before you build on it.

Who it is for
A team with a model, analysis or benchmark result it plans to rely on.
What I do
Rerun the claim from your artifacts, add the adversarial controls it needs, and state what survives.
You receive
A written audit with every control's result, a reproducible script, and a plain verdict.
Scope
One claim, one system, up to four weeks.
$6,000 – $12,000
OFFER 02

Preregistered study

Find out what an intervention does before you ship it.

Who it is for
A team evaluating a steering, patching, ablation or protocol change.
What I do
Write and register the hypothesis and analysis plan first, then run the study exactly as registered.
You receive
The registered protocol, run logs, and a results report with controls and limitations, including any reversal.
Scope
One hypothesis, one comparator set, up to eight weeks.
$15,000 – $30,000
OFFER 03

Measurement instrument

A tool that shows how much of the picture survived.

Who it is for
A team whose system dynamics are described but never directly observed.
What I do
Build an instrument on your data that runs offline, with a fidelity figure reported on every view.
You receive
A working tool, tests, a fidelity note for each view, and handover documentation.
Scope
One system, one instrument, up to six weeks.
$20,000 – $40,000

How it works

Send a paragraph describing the claim, system or question. I reply within a week saying which offer fits, or that none does. Prices are fixed ranges for the scope above, confirmed in writing before any work starts. Expenses and taxes are not included.

I do not guarantee a result, and I do not take on work for surveillance, targeting, or security testing without authorisation. The terms apply to everything produced here.
