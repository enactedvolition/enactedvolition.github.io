3. The research is research

Everything here is exploratory work published as it develops. Results are reported with their controls and their failures, including the ones that went against my own predictions — but they are single studies, not settled findings, and some have not been peer reviewed.

In particular, the computational neuropharmacology work is a simulation of a simplified model of dopaminergic signalling. It is not medical or clinical guidance, it makes no claim about any real person, and nothing on this site should be used to inform a decision about drugs, treatment or health. Speak to a qualified clinician.

4. The security tools

GHOST_CHAIN, WHISPER_KEY and NET_INTERCEPT are archived instruments, provided for research, education and authorised testing.

By using them you confirm that you have permission for every target you point them at, and that your use complies with the law where you are. Reconnaissance against systems you do not own or have written authorisation to test is unlawful in most jurisdictions. I am not responsible for what you do with them.

They also have real limits. Browser CORS rules block much of what a server-side tool could reach, several panels return guidance rather than live data, and the steganography tool's output is not a secure channel — LSB embedding is detectable by standard steganalysis regardless of how the payload is encrypted. Treat their results as indicative and verify anything that matters with a proper tool.
